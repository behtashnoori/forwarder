==================================================
4. PRODUCT DECISION — STOP PLACEMENT
==================================================

The Product Owner explicitly resolves:

P311_STOP_PLACEMENT=ARRIVAL_POINT_BEFORE_NEXT_MOVEMENT

Meaning:

Stop / Operation time associated with a Route Leg belongs to the ARRIVAL point
of that leg.

Its effect is on CONTINUING BEYOND that point.

==================================================
5. ETA TO THE ARRIVAL POINT
==================================================

For:

A → B

with:

movement(A→B)
+
stop/operation(B)

ETA ARRIVAL AT B is:

movement(A→B)

Do NOT include stop/operation(B) in the ETA for arriving at B.

The stop begins conceptually after arrival at B.

==================================================
6. ETA TO A LATER POINT
==================================================

For:

A → B → C

ETA(B):

movement(A→B)

ETA(C):

movement(A→B)
+
remaining stop/operation(B), if not proven complete
+
movement(B→C)

Continue the same logic across later intermediate points.

==================================================
7. FINAL DESTINATION
==================================================

Final ETA is:

ARRIVAL ETA.

Do NOT include stop/operation AFTER arrival at the final destination.

Therefore:

arrival at final destination
≠
delivery completion
≠
unloading completion
≠
customs completion
≠
operational closure.

P3-11 does NOT create:

Delivery ETA
Customs ETA
Operational Completion ETA.

==================================================
8. INTERMEDIATE STOP NOT PROVEN COMPLETE
==================================================

If arrival at B is proven:

but completion of required stop/operation(B) is NOT proven:

the entire configured remaining stop/operation range for B remains part of the
ETA to the next point.

Example:

reference stop(B) = 4–8 hours

Cargo arrived at B two hours ago.

Do NOT automatically change remaining stop to:

2–6 hours.

Elapsed wall-clock time is NOT evidence of operation progress.

No prorating.

==================================================
9. STOP COMPLETION PROOF
==================================================

Stop/operation(B) is considered consumed for remaining ETA only when a governed
fact proves that its required operation has completed.

A governed start of the NEXT movement may also prove that the required
pre-departure stop at B has already been completed.

Use existing explicit operational semantics only.

Do NOT infer completion from:

- free text;
- elapsed time;
- location text;
- estimated speed;
- unrelated ExecutionUnit;
- another Cargo branch.

==================================================
10. ZERO VS UNKNOWN
==================================================

Explicit:

0 / 0

means:

no reference stop duration.

NULL / missing:

means unknown.

NULL is NEVER converted to zero.

If a required intermediate stop has no governed duration and no fact proves it
complete:

the dependent ETA is unavailable.
